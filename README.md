# Dynamic-Gait-Stability-Between-Overground-and-Treadmill-Walking-in-Parkinson-s-Disease
Time-continuous statistical parametric mapping (SPM) analyses.

Although dynamic gait stability is known to vary across gait cycle phases, most studies have relied on discrete analyses, and phase-specific patterns remain untested with time-continuous analyses. This study compared dynamic gait stability between overground walking (OG) and treadmill walking (TM) at self-selected speeds in people with Parkinson's disease (PwPD) using discrete analysis and statistical parametric mapping (SPM), including gait-phase-normalised (GPN) SPM. Sixteen PwPD completed both OG and TM conditions. Feasible stability region (FSR)-based dynamic gait stability, centre of mass (COM) position, and COM velocity were analysed as discrete values and continuous time series.
